title: Partner Intelligence
tagline: Prioritize partnerships with criteria and sources, not with a model ranking
summary: An invitation-only platform for researching and prioritizing strategic partnerships with traceable evidence.
problem: Choosing a partnership without shared criteria, weights, and traceable sources mixes opinion with evidence.
whyItMatters: As a Data & BI Fellowship contribution at No Country, the work focused on defining and validating evaluation criteria, source evidence, weights, and analytical decision logic — not on claiming sole authorship of the whole product.
solution: An invitation-only research and prioritization platform that scores partner fit and program opportunity separately and requires explicit review when evidence is incomplete.
approach: Scoring uses deterministic AHP/TOPSIS. The language model does not create numerical ranks. Partner fit and program opportunity are scored as separate dimensions so the recommendation remains explainable from criteria and evidence.
engineeringDecisions:
  - Incomplete evidence triggers explicit review with traceable source references.
  - Partner fit and program opportunity are scored separately.
  - Numerical ranks come from deterministic multi-criteria methods, not from LLM output.
  - Invite-only access is not presented as a public demo.
validation: Criteria and weights are structured so a decision can be explained from evidence rather than from opaque model scores.
benefits:
  - Supports partnership prioritization with traceable evidence.
  - Keeps ranking explainable through criteria and weights.
  - Frames contribution as fellowship-scoped decision support, not sole product ownership.
limitations: Access uses a commercial invite login; the UI may have changed since these screenshots. The documented contribution is limited to the fellowship scope.
