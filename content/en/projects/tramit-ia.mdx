## Problem

Information on procedures of the municipality and its sub-municipalities is not gathered into guidance a person can consult.

## Solution

A website that answers with the catalog's published fact sheets.

## Approach

If the procedure is not in the catalog, the system says so and searches the internet. In-person verification remains in the editorial work.
