## Problem

Inventory planning needs demand forecasts from transactional history, but raw sales tables are not model-ready and model choice is easy to leave unverified.

## Solution

An ETL and feature pipeline feeds an XGBoost versus Prophet comparison, with FastAPI inference over the trained path and Streamlit for exploration.
